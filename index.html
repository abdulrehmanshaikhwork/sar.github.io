Featured project

IT Services Business Performance & Client Analytics Dashboard

Excel MySQL SQL Power BI DAX

The scope

An end-to-end analytics solution across 5 relational tables covering 180 unique clients, 150 employees, 350 projects, and 1,800 support tickets — analyzing profitability, delivery performance, SLA compliance, customer experience, and workforce productivity.

Data quality

Designed the relational data model in MySQL and ran data-quality checks: identified 2 duplicate client records (182 raw rows → 180 unique clients) and classified 4 records with missing Industry values as "Unknown" rather than assuming values.

Analysis

Wrote SQL queries — multi-table joins, aggregations, filtering — and Power BI DAX measures to calculate core business KPIs: total profit (~$75.9M), profit margin (~32.7%), late projects (149, ~42.6% of total), SLA breach %, average resolution time, and employee hours utilization.

Dashboard & reporting

Built a Power BI data model with relationships across all five tables and designed a five-page interactive dashboard — Executive Overview, Project Analytics, IT Support Analytics, Employee Analytics, and Management Insights — with KPI cards, slicers, and drillable visuals, using AI-assisted reporting to translate outputs into concise, management-focused recommendations.
